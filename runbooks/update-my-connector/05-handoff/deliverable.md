# Deliverable: 05-handoff

This is the definition of done for the maker. It says what good looks like.
It does not say how the work is tested. Keep tests out of this file.

## Hand in

`owner-sheet.md`.

## Format

"Changed in this run" first (each change, who decided, result), then every section of the build's owner sheet, current.

## Must be true

- Every change from `changes.md` appears with its result.
- Every connection and harness shows its result from `04-resmoke`.
- The baseline points at this run's inventory.
- The smoke key's revocation is recorded.
- No value appears anywhere.

## Out of scope

- Doing any item under "Still to do".
