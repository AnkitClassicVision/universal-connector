# Route

Pick the runbook whose "Use when" matches the request.

- One match: open that runbook's `SKILL.md`.
- No match, or more than one: stop and hand the request to the registry steward. Never guess.

| Use when | Runbook |
|---|---|
| Someone wants to add, adopt, change or retire a process | `runbooks/registry-steward` |
| Someone wants to gather their existing connectors, keys, logins and skills into one portable door and plug it into their agent harnesses | `runbooks/build-my-connector` |
| Someone already has a door and wants to add, remove or rotate connections or harnesses, or re-check it | `runbooks/update-my-connector` |
